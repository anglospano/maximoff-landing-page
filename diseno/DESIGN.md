## Brand & Style

This design system channels an unapologetically energetic, warm, and deeply human presence. Drawing direct cues from the psych-fluid lettering and vibrant scarlet outlines of the logo identity, the aesthetic merges the raw expressiveness of youth advocacy with structured clarity. It strips away clinical sterility in favor of visceral connection, warmth, and relentless community optimism.

The design movement balances **High-Contrast / Bold** impact with **Warm Humanist Modernism**:
- **Target Audience:** Young adults, adolescents, mental wellness advocates, peer mentors, and community organizers.
- **Emotional Response:** Empathetic, fearless, grounded, invigorating, and stigma-free. The UI should make seeking mental health support feel like joining an inspiring, creative movement rather than entering an institutional clinic.
- **Visual Stance:** Stark, deliberate contrast where saturated vermillion red cuts through deep carbon surfaces and clean, warm ivory neutrals, backed by fluid, dynamic accents.

## Colors

The color palette centers around the fiery kinetic red of the emblem, paired with grounding carbon tones and breathable warm neutrals:

- **Primary (`#E50914`):** The primary pulse. Used for high-stakes calls-to-action, active community alerts, energetic focal points, and impactful branding borders.
- **Secondary (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep carbon charcoal. Replaces default black with an ink-rich, velvety neutral for headlines, heavy buttons, key structural surfaces, and protective contrast against vibrant accents.
- **Tertiary (`#FF4D4D`):** Warm electric poppy. Deployed for subtle state highlights, hover interactions, supportive badge backgrounds, and radiant interactive moments.
- **Neutral (`#F9F7F5`):** Soft milk/warm canvas. Provides an inviting, glare-free, hospitable surface that softens stark contrasts and avoids the harsh, sterile tone of pure `#FFFFFF`.

### Functional Usage
- **Emergency / Crisis Support:** Reserved exclusively for bright crimson `#E50914` on high-contrast `#1A1A1A` banners to ensure immediate affordance.
- **Surfaces:** Default surface uses `#F9F7F5`, elevating with layered containers in pure white `#FFFFFF` or elevated dark carbon cards `#222222` for hero moments.
- **Text:** Primary text anchors at `#1A1A1A`, secondary meta-labels at `#555555`, and reverse text in crisp off-white `#F9F7F5`.

## Typography

Typography embodies two crucial personalities: **artistic rebellion and expressive courage** paired with **clarity, comfort, and cognitive ease**.

- **Display & Headline Font (`Syne`):** Echoes the flowing, punchy, uninhibited curves of the foundational wordmark. Used for statement titles, mission banners, statistics, and inspiring callouts.
- **Body & Label Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, inviting, and open. Its tall x-height and friendly geometric forms ensure effortless reading across resources, peer chat interfaces, clinical guides, and intake forms.

Titles utilize tight letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic poster design aesthetics, while smaller labels utilize generous uppercase tracking (`0.05em`) for immediate recognition.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt dynamic fluid grid system that flexes comfortably from pocket mobile devices to large display viewports:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers standardly bound at a maximum reading width of 1240px for community portals.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Secondary content stacks beneath narrative anchors.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas margin (`margin-mobile`) and `1rem` column gutters (`gutter-mobile`). Bottom navigation docks ensure thumb-friendly crisis access.

Spacious internal padding (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) inside modules gives sensitive content room to breathe, preventing claustrophobic or anxiety-inducing information density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons cold, floaty drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Dual-Border Framing** and **Tinted Ambient Warmth**:

- **Low Elevation (Cards, Tiles):** No artificial blur. Elevation is achieved via solid structural borders (`1.5px solid #1A1A1A` or `1.5px solid rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.15)`) over pure white surfaces `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by an offset tint-shadow: `0 4px 0px 0px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`.
- **Medium Elevation (Active Modals, Interactive Popovers):** A crisp, bold outline paired with a warm, subtle shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`.
- **High Focus (Emergency Floating Actions):** Pure carbon surfaces `#1A1A1A` edged in vibrant scarlet `#E50914` glowing outlines: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #E50914, 0 12px 32px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape geometry embraces roundedness level `2` (`0.5rem` / 8px baseline radius), transitioning to pill formations on high-engagement elements:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Rounded-lg (`1rem` / 16px) corners soften the visual weight of heavy dark borders, projecting an approachable, non-institutional demeanor.
- **Buttons, Tags, & Chips:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) styling communicates safety, tactile ease, and frictionless interaction.
- **Graphic Motif:** Accent containers occasionally feature the signature fluid outline framing seen in the wordmark, using soft, wavy container caps for editorial hero units.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Saturated `#E50914` background, `#FFFFFF` bold text, pill-shaped (`9999px`), with `padding: 0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover triggers a subtle scale lift (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) and deepens to `#C90812` with a carbon bottom-ridge shadow.
- **Secondary / Action:** Solid `#1A1A1A` background with crisp white typography. Hover introduces an energetic `#E50914` ring outline.
- **Emergency Helpline Button:** Floating pill with dark carbon `#1A1A1A` fill, vibrant scarlet `#E50914` border, pulsating tertiary glow, and high-legibility label.

### Cards
- Surface is `#FFFFFF` resting against the `#F9F7F5` foundation. Finished with a clean `1.5px` border in `#EBE8E4` that shifts to `#E50914` on card hover. Padding remains generous (`1.5rem` to `2rem`). Featured impact stories feature a top scarlet accent lip (`4px` height).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Rendered in pill format. Unselected: transparent background with a gentle `#D8D4CE` outline and `#1A1A1A` label. Selected: filled `#1A1A1A` with `#FFFFFF` text or filled `#FFE8E8` with `#E50914` bold text.

### Form Inputs
- Background is `#FFFFFF` with an inset padding of `0.875rem 1rem` and `0.5rem` border radius. Inactive border is `1.5px solid #D8D4CE`. Focus state transitions to `1.5px solid #E50914` with a gentle `3px` red halo (`rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded-sm (`4px`) for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Active selection features `#E50914` fill with a sharp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip. Inactive states feature a distinct `#1A1A1A` outline.

### Community & Crisis Badges
- High-contrast, compact tokens with bold all-caps `label-sm` typography. Crisis badges use `#E50914` with pure `#FFFFFF` text; community status chips use `#1A1A1A` with `#F9F7F5` text.
